I do not want this project to be a typical e-commerce application where customers browse products and purchase individual items.
The purpose of this platform is to create a B2B product sourcing and procurement platform connecting businesses and customers in Ethiopia with manufacturers and suppliers in China.
The core idea is that products listed on the platform are primarily products that can be manufactured or sourced in bulk from China. Customers should therefore be able to discover products, review their specifications and minimum order quantities, and express interest in purchasing large quantities such as 100, 200, 500, 1,000, or more units.
The platform should focus on product discovery, bulk purchasing, supplier coordination, quotation requests, communication, documentation, and order management, rather than functioning as a conventional online store.
The platform will have an agent based sourcing model.
We will have an agent located in China who will communicate directly with manufacturers and suppliers. The agent will collect product information, pricing, manufacturing details, images, videos, minimum order quantities, available customization options, production timelines, and other relevant information.
The agent will then publish and maintain these products through the platform.
Customers and businesses in Ethiopia will be able to browse the available products and evaluate whether a product is suitable for their business.
For each product, the platform should clearly display information such as:
- Product name
- Product description
- Product category
- Product images and videos
- Product specifications
- Available variants
- Minimum order quantity (MOQ)
- Estimated unit price or price range
- Bulk pricing tiers
- Available customization options
- Material and manufacturing information
- Estimated production time
- Estimated shipping information
- Supplier/manufacturer information where appropriate
- Product status
- Date the product information was last updated
The customer-facing application should provide a modern product discovery experience.
Users should be able to:
- Browse products by category
- Search for products
- Filter products by MOQ
- Filter by price range
- Filter by category
- Filter by customization availability
- Sort products
- Save/favorite products
- Compare products where appropriate
- View detailed product information
- Request more information about a product
- Request a quotation
- Specify the quantity they are interested in purchasing
The quantity selector should be designed specifically for bulk orders, rather than individual retail purchases.
For example, instead of simply showing:
Quantity: 1
the system should make it clear that the product may have a minimum order quantity such as:
Minimum order quantity: 500 units
and allow the customer to request quantities such as 500, 1,000, 2,000, or a custom quantity.
The customer should not necessarily purchase the product directly through a traditional checkout system.
Instead, after finding a product they are interested in, the customer should be able to initiate a product inquiry or quotation request.
The customer can provide:
- Product
- Desired quantity
- Required customization
- Target budget, if applicable
- Delivery destination
- Additional requirements
- Attachments or reference images
- Additional comments
The request should then be sent to the appropriate customer support or sourcing team.
The agent or support team can communicate with the customer, confirm the requirements, negotiate pricing with the manufacturer, and eventually provide a quotation.
The quotation process should support multiple stages, such as:
1. Inquiry submitted
2. Inquiry under review
3. Supplier contacted
4. Supplier quotation received
5. Customer quotation prepared
6. Negotiation
7. Quotation accepted
8. Order confirmed
9. Production
10. Quality inspection
11. Shipping
12. Delivered
13. Completed
The system should maintain the entire history of this process.
Customers should have multiple ways to contact the support team.
Depending on the available infrastructure, this may include:
- Email
- Phone/voice calls
- Telegram
- In-platform messaging
- Potentially WhatsApp or other communication channels in the future
The customer-facing application should clearly expose the available support channels.
For example, a customer could see:
- Contact support
- Call support
- Chat on Telegram
- Send an email
- Request a callback
The platform should also keep communication and inquiry information associated with the customer's account and relevant order or quotation.

Because this is a B2B sourcing platform, the system needs proper document management.
Some transactions may require customers to sign agreements, contracts, purchase documents, specifications, quotations, or other business documents.
The platform should therefore support:

Documents should be stored securely and access should be controlled according to the user's role and relationship to the transaction.

The most important thing is that this application should not be designed as a normal e-commerce marketplace.
The core workflow is:
Discover a product → Evaluate bulk requirements → Submit an inquiry → Communicate with sourcing/support → Receive a quotation → Agree on terms → Sign documents → Make payment → Production → Quality inspection → Shipping → Delivery
The application should support this entire business process.
Before implementing individual features, the architecture and database design should be modeled around this workflow so that the platform represents a real-world China-to-Ethiopia B2B sourcing operation, rather than simply representing an online product catalog.
